Why the town matters for plumbing

Water on the Upper Cape is not one system. Bourne, Barnstable and Mashpee each have separate districts, building departments and rules about permits, which affects who you call and what paperwork a repair needs. We know where the lines are, so you do not have to sort it out in the middle of an emergency.
