Business Plumbing Emergency? Start Here

If a restroom is flooding, a kitchen line is backing up or a water heater has failed, the first move is to stop the water and call. Know where your main shutoff valve is and make sure staff know it too. In many small buildings it is near the water meter or where the line enters the basement.

Call the emergency line and tell us the business type, what is affected and whether customers are on site. We schedule so you can reopen as soon as it is safe.

What to do before help arrives

In a shop, office or rental, the first few minutes protect people, stock and the building.

  • Shut the nearest fixture valve or the main water valve.
  • Keep customers and staff out of wet areas and put out signs.
  • Move inventory and electronics off the floor if it is safe.
  • Do not use drains that are backing up.
  • Take photos and note times for your records and your landlord.

Commercial Versus Residential Plumbing

The basics are the same. The scale and the consequences are not. Commercial lines carry more fixtures, kitchens add grease, and public restrooms see more wear. A failure also has a business cost beyond the repair.

Drains, Grease And Floor Drains

Kitchens and food prep areas build grease on pipe walls. Regular cleaning keeps drains open and reduces emergency calls. For stubborn buildup we use hydro jetting to scour the lines. Floor drains in back rooms and basements also collect debris and are worth including in a maintenance visit.

If a grease trap or interceptor is part of your setup, the proper maintenance schedule depends on the type and local rules. Ask the Sandwich Health Department what applies to your business.

Working In Sandwich

Sandwich has three villages, Sandwich, East Sandwich and Forestdale, and a lot of businesses depend on the summer crowd. A closed restroom or a dead water heater in July is a real loss. We schedule around your hours where we can.

For permits, the Sandwich Building Department handles plumbing and gas inspections through its Permit Eyes portal. Inspections run on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays and are booked by phone. Ask the Building Department before starting any installation or renovation. Plumbing permits are issued only to licensed plumbers.

Source: Town of Sandwich Building Department and the Cape Cod Chamber of Commerce.

Planned Maintenance To Avoid The Next Emergency

Most business plumbing emergencies have warning signs. A slow drain, a tank that groans, a faint drip under a sink. A short maintenance visit finds them while they are cheap.

Reactive approach

  • Wait for the backup during service hours
  • Pay after hours rates for a rush job
  • Lose sales while the room is closed

Planned approach

  • Clean drains before busy season
  • Flush and test water heaters
  • Exercise shutoff valves so they work when needed
