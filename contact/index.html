How to reach us

Call (508) 389-4045 for anything urgent. For non-urgent questions, email contact@proplumbingsandwich.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Our address is 65 Route 6A, Sandwich, MA 02563. Please call before visiting so we know a plumber is there to meet you.

What to tell us when you call

Give your address and what you see or hear. Mention whether water is running, whether anyone is at risk and when the problem started. If you can shut off the water, do that first, then call.

Towns we cover

We serve Sandwich, Bourne, Barnstable and Mashpee. If you are unsure whether your address is in range, call and ask. See the service areas page for ZIP codes.
