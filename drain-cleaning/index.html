A Backed-Up Drain Is Urgent When Water Has Nowhere To Go

If water is rising in a sink, bubbling up in a tub when you flush, or leaking from a basement floor drain, call now. A blockage that has reached the main line will not clear on its own, and every flush or load of laundry adds more water to the problem.

Drain cleaning in Sandwich covers everything from a single slow kitchen sink to a full main line stoppage. The first job on the phone is working out which kind of clog you have, because a clog in one fixture and a clog in the main line need different tools and a different price.

  • Stop running water in the house until we have talked it through.
  • Do not pour chemical drain openers into a standing blockage. They sit in the pipe and burn skin and gaskets when a plumber opens the line.
  • Move rugs, boxes and stored items away from basement floor drains.
  • Note which fixtures are affected. That tells us how far down the line the clog sits.

Where Drain Clogs Actually Start

Most clogs form in the same few places. A kitchen sink clogs where grease cools and clings to the first bend under the cabinet. A bathroom sink clogs in the trap, where hair and soap film build into a mat. A tub or shower clogs just past the strainer, where hair wraps around the crossbar.

A main line clog is different. It sits beyond every branch, so it shows up as slow drains everywhere or as water backing up at the lowest opening in the house. Common causes are tree roots finding a joint, paper and wipes that snag on a rough spot, and years of scale narrowing the pipe.

How We Clear A Drain

The method depends on what the clog is made of and how far into the system it sits. We start with the least invasive tool that will work, then step up only if the line needs it.

For sinks and tubs we usually begin by pulling the trap and checking for a simple blockage. If the trap is clear, a small drum cable goes down the branch line to break up the obstruction and pull it back out. Kitchen lines often need a cutting head because grease hardens into a ring that a plain cable simply pokes through.

Main line machines

A main line stoppage calls for a larger electric cable machine run through the cleanout. Heavier cutting heads open the pipe to its full diameter instead of just punching a hole through the clog. When a line keeps clogging after a basic clearing, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water.

Camera follow-up

After a main line job, a camera run shows whether the pipe is actually clear and whether something else is going on, such as a cracked joint or a belly where water pools. If the camera finds damage, we explain what it shows and whether sewer line repair makes sense.

Cable Machine Or Jetting: Picking The Right Method

We tell you which method fits your line before work starts, and the price is clear before we begin.

Cable machine

  • Best for a first-time stoppage in a sink, tub or main line
  • Cuts through roots and soft blockages quickly
  • Lower cost for a simple clog
  • May leave a film on the pipe wall that lets the clog return

Hydro jetting

  • Best for grease, scale and repeat clogs
  • Cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a channel
  • Useful after roots have been cut out
  • Not suited to fragile or badly cracked pipe

Drain Cleaning In Sandwich Homes

Sandwich is a town of mostly owner-occupied homes, about 89 percent according to the Census Bureau, and many of them are older Cape-style houses with drain lines that have seen decades of use. Older cast iron and clay lines roughen with age, which gives paper and grease more to catch on.

Whether your street connects to a municipal sewer or uses a Title 5 septic system changes what a clog means. A stoppage in a septic home can point at the house line, the septic tank outlet or the leaching field. Ask the Sandwich Health Department whether your street has sewer or a Title 5 system, and tell us which you have when you call.

Keeping Drains Clear After We Leave

A few habits keep a cleared drain clear. Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing, and put cooking fat in a can instead of down the sink. Use a hair catcher in every tub and shower. Flush only toilet paper, because wipes labeled flushable still tangle in older lines.

If the same drain clogs more than a couple of times a year, the clog is probably not the real problem. A belly in the pipe, a root intrusion or a failing joint keeps catching debris. That is the point where a camera inspection saves money compared with repeated clearing.

Other Plumbing Problems That Look Like A Clog

Not every slow drain is a clog. A blocked vent stack makes drains gurgle and drain slowly because air cannot enter the system behind the water. A broken or sagging section of pipe holds water in a low spot and clogs again within days. A failing septic tank can back up into the house and look exactly like a main line clog.

If a kitchen sink backs up into the dishwasher, the disposal may be the culprit, and garbage disposal repair may be the fix. If a toilet will not flush but nothing else is slow, see our toilet repair service.
