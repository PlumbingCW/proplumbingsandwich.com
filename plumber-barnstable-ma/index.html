Questions Barnstable Homeowners Ask Us

Barnstable is the largest town on Cape Cod by area and includes Hyannis, Centerville, Osterville, Cotuit, Marstons Mills, West Barnstable and Barnstable Village. Calls usually begin with the same questions. Who do I call first, how fast can someone get here and what will it cost. This page answers those the way we do on the phone.

Our office is in Sandwich, so we are a short drive from West Barnstable and the northern villages and a longer drive to the south side. We never promise a fixed arrival time, but we tell you what we expect when you call.

What Counts As An Emergency

An emergency is anything that threatens your home or your health right now. Water spraying from a pipe, sewage backing into a floor drain, a flooded basement and no heat from a boiler in winter all qualify. So does a gas smell, though for gas you should leave the building and call the gas utility and 911 before calling a plumber.

Slow drips, a running toilet or a clog in one sink can usually wait for the next day. If you are unsure, call the line and describe it. We would rather help you decide than have you guess.

Who Supplies Your Water

Water supply in Barnstable is split among several providers, including the Barnstable Fire District, the Centerville-Osterville-Marstons Mills water district, the Cotuit Fire District water department and the Hyannis Water System. Your street determines the provider.

In an emergency, the street shutoff belongs to your supplier. Call them if you cannot stop water inside the house. Our plumbers can help you find the main shutoff inside, and then we handle the repair.

Common Barnstable Plumbing Jobs

Beyond emergencies, Barnstable homeowners call about aging water heaters, slow drains and hidden leaks. Many homes in the villages are older and some properties use septic systems, where sewer line and Title 5 questions come up. Septic rules fall under 310 CMR 15.000 and the Board of Health can advise on those.

For leaks that show up as a high water bill, leak detection is the right first step. For lines that back up repeatedly, our sewer line cleaning and sewer inspection pages explain the options. A failing water heater is covered on our water heater replacement page.

We are licensed and insured, and you can verify any Massachusetts plumber through the state's eLIPSE portal. Permits for plumbing work in Barnstable go through the town building department, so ask the Town of Barnstable what applies to your job.
