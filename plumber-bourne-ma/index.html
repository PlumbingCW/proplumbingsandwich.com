Emergency Plumbing Across Bourne's Villages

Bourne is not one neighborhood. It is a string of villages that stretches from Buzzards Bay on the mainland side of the Cape Cod Canal to Sagamore and Sagamore Beach on the Cape side, with Monument Beach, Pocasset and Cataumet along Buzzards Bay. When you call our emergency line from any of them, we ask which village you are in so we can plan the route and the timing.

We work from Sandwich on Route 6A, which puts us close to the Sagamore end of town. We do not promise an arrival time because canal bridge and Route 6 traffic can change a trip quickly. We tell you what we expect when you call, and we keep you updated.

Emergencies We Are Called For In Bourne

These are the situations that lead to most of our late-night calls in town.

Burst or frozen pipes

Cold snaps near the water can freeze pipes in unheated crawl spaces and seasonal homes. See our burst pipe repair page for what to do before we arrive.

Sewer backups

A main line that backs up into a tub or floor drain is a health risk and needs fast clearing. We use cable machines and camera checks, and our sewer line repair team handles damaged pipe.

No hot water

A failed tank in winter is an emergency for a family. Our water heater repair work covers tank and tankless units.

Hidden leaks

A sudden jump in the water bill or a wet spot on a ceiling points to a leak behind the walls. Leak detection finds it without guesswork.

Water Districts In Bourne

Bourne is served by several water districts, including the Bourne, Buzzards Bay and North Sagamore districts. Which one supplies your home depends on your street. Knowing your district matters in an emergency because the shutoff at the street belongs to the district, not to us.

If you cannot stop water inside the house, call the district that serves your address and then call us. We can help you find your own main shutoff if you are unsure where it is.

Source: Town of Bourne water district listings.

Seasonal And Older Homes

A large share of Bourne's homes sit near the water and many are seasonal or used part of the year. Houses that sit empty through winter can freeze, flood from a failed supply hose or leak for weeks before anyone notices. If you own a second home, ask about a shutoff and a drain-down plan before the cold sets in.

Older houses also bring older plumbing, from galvanized supply lines to cast iron drains. When we find failing pipe during an emergency visit, we explain whether a repair holds or whether repiping is worth planning.

Before We Arrive

A few quick steps limit damage while you wait for the plumber.

Shut off the water

Turn the main shutoff clockwise. If you do not know where it is, tell us and we will walk you through finding it.

Kill power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, switch off that circuit only if you can reach it safely.

Move belongings

Lift rugs, boxes and furniture off wet floors and open cabinets under leaking sinks.

Take photos

Photograph the damage for your insurer before you start cleaning up.

Licensed Work And Permits

Plumbing in Massachusetts is licensed work. The Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters licenses plumbers, and you can verify any license through the state's eLIPSE portal. Permits for plumbing work go through the building department in your town, so ask the Town of Bourne what it needs for your address.

Our work follows 248 CMR 10.00, the state plumbing code. Emergency repairs that need a permit are done properly and we explain the process so nothing surprises you later.
