Main Line Backup: Do These Things Right Now

If wastewater is coming up through a floor drain, a tub or a basement toilet, treat it as urgent and call the line now. A blocked main means nothing in the house can drain. The longer water keeps running, the more ends up on the floor.

Here is what helps while you wait. Shut off washing machines and dishwashers. Stop flushing. If the overflow is near electrical outlets or a panel, stay clear and turn off power only if you can reach the breaker without stepping in water.

2

Say which fixtures are backing up. Multiple fixtures point to the main line, one fixture points to a branch.

How We Tell A Main Line Clog From A Fixture Clog

A single slow sink is usually a branch problem and a short cable run fixes it. When a basement floor drain bubbles as an upstairs toilet flushes, or the lowest fixture in the house backs up first, the stoppage is in the main line and is farther downstream.

That difference matters because main line work needs a bigger machine, access through a cleanout and often a camera. We figure it out on the phone so the right equipment is on the truck.

Common Causes Behind A Blocked Sewer Line

Tree roots

Roots find tiny gaps at pipe joints and grow into the line. Over time they form a net that catches paper.

Grease and wipes

Fats harden and wipes snag. Together they build up into a mass that narrows the pipe.

Belly in the pipe

A low spot holds water and solids. The line may clean out and then back up again.

Broken or offset pipe

A crushed or separated section catches debris. Cleaning helps, but the pipe needs repair.

What Happens During A Cleaning Visit

We start by finding the cleanout and checking the line with a camera when conditions allow. A sectional or drum cable machine with a cutting head clears the stoppage and trims roots back to the pipe wall. When buildup is heavy, we follow with hydro jetting to scour the walls.

Before we leave, we run water through the line and confirm it flows. If we find damage, you see it on the camera and hear your options before any repair work is quoted.

Cleaning Or Repair? A Plain Comparison

Cleaning is enough when

  • The camera shows a clean, intact pipe after clearing
  • The cause was grease, paper or a one time blockage
  • Flow is normal when we test it
  • No standing water remains in the pipe

Repair comes next when

  • The camera shows a crack, collapse or offset joint
  • Roots return quickly to the same spot
  • A belly holds water between flushes
  • The line backs up repeatedly after cleaning

What Sandwich Homeowners Should Know

Sandwich has about 20,575 residents by the Census Bureau's 2025 estimate, and roughly 89 percent of homes are owner occupied. Many properties are older and many sit on large lots, which can mean long runs between the house and the street or tank.

Not every Sandwich property has a municipal sewer. The Town of Sandwich Health Department is the place to ask whether your street has sewer or a Title 5 septic system. If you are on septic, a backup can also come from a full tank or a saturated field, and cleaning the house line will not fix that. We will tell you if the symptoms point that way.

Habits That Keep The Main Line Open

  • Throw cooking grease in the trash once it cools.
  • Flush only toilet paper. Wipes marked flushable still snag.
  • Run a camera look every so often on an older line, especially if you have large trees near it.
  • Keep the cleanout cap accessible and not buried or blocked by storage.
  • Call at the first gurgle. Small warning signs are cheaper than a floor full of sewage.
