How A Tankless Water Heater Works

A tankless heater heats water only when you open a tap. A flow sensor detects the demand, the burner or element fires and the water leaves hot. There is no storage tank to keep warm, which is where the potential energy savings and the compact size come from.

The tradeoff is that the unit must deliver a large amount of heat in a short moment. That is why gas supply, venting and water quality matter far more here than they do with a tank.

What Has To Be Right For An Install

Homes that already have a tank heater often need changes before a tankless unit will run well. We check each of these before quoting.

  • Gas line size. Many tankless units draw far more gas than a tank, so an older half-inch line may need upgrading.
  • Venting. Tankless units usually use sealed stainless or plastic vent pipe with specific length limits.
  • Water line and shutoffs. Service valves let you flush the unit later without a long job.
  • Condensate drain. High-efficiency units produce acidic condensate that must drain correctly.
  • Electrical outlet. Most units need power for the controls, fans and igniters.

Flushing And Descaling

Mineral scale builds on the heat exchanger and slowly reduces flow and efficiency. Annual flushing with a descaling solution is the standard care for most tankless units and manufacturers often require it to keep a warranty valid.

A flush takes a pump, hoses and service valves and usually runs for around an hour. If your unit has no service valves, we can add them so future flushes are simple. Skipping the flush is the single most common reason a tankless heater starts throwing error codes.

When A Tankless Heater Is Not The Right Move

A tankless unit is not always the best answer. A small home with low hot water use may never earn back the added install cost. A property with long, uninsulated pipe runs will still wait for hot water to arrive.

We will say so if a tank or a hybrid makes more sense. For a standard swap, see our water heater installation page. If your tankless unit has failed and you have no hot water, our water heater repair team handles urgent calls.
